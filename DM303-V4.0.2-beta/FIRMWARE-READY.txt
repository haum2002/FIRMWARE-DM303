INSTALLATION STEPS:
-------------------
1. Format SD Card to FAT32 (SDHC recommended, 4GB-32GB ideal)
2. Copy ALL files and folders from this directory to SD Card root
3. Ensure the /system folder is copied completely
4. Power OFF your DM303 multimeter
5. Insert SD Card into the device
6. Press and HOLD the UPDATE/OK button
7. While holding, power ON the device
8. Continue holding until update screen appears
9. Wait for completion (DO NOT interrupt power!)
10. Remove SD Card and reboot device

VERIFICATION:
-------------
After installation, check:
- Display shows "V4.0.2b" in about screen
- No freezing during normal operation
- Stable readings near noise sources (phone chargers, etc.)
- All buttons respond correctly
